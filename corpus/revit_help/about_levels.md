# About Levels

Levels are finite horizontal planes that act as a reference for level-hosted elements, such as roofs, floors, and ceilings.

One should establish a level for each known story or other necessary reference point within the building (such as first floor, top of wall, or bottom of foundation).

To incorporate levels, you must be working within a section or elevation view. Upon adding levels, you have the option to generate an associated plan view.

The extents of a level can be adjusted to prevent their appearance in certain views. Additional information is available under datum extents and visibility documentation.

Level annotations may be concealed after insertion. Refer to resources about hiding elements in views for further guidance.
